DEV3 AI Smart Coupons & Dynamic Discounts

Detect shoppers about to abandon their cart and show them a personal, single-use offer with a countdown. AI picks the offer, and it always stays within your margin rules. Version 1.0.0 · by DEV3 Technologies.

Installation

  1. Make sure WooCommerce (7.6 or newer) is installed and active. The plugin shows a notice and stays idle without it.
  2. In your Envato download, find dev3-ai-smart-coupons-for-woocommerce.zip. This is the installable plugin; do not upload the whole bundle.
  3. Go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the zip, click Install Now, then Activate.
  4. A new DEV3 AI Smart Coupons menu appears in the admin sidebar.

Requirements: WordPress 6.0+, PHP 7.4–8.3 with the OpenSSL extension, and WooCommerce 7.6+ (tested with 11.2, HPOS and Cart & Checkout blocks). AI offers also need outbound HTTPS to generativelanguage.googleapis.com.

Quick start (5 minutes)

  1. Rules. Set the maximum percentage (for example 15%) and maximum fixed amount, your minimum spend and a daily discount budget. Enter the cost meta key your cost plugin uses (for example _wc_cog_cost) and a minimum margin.
  2. Simulator (bottom of Rules). Try a shopper, such as a $120 cart with exit intent, and see the risk score, why it scored that way, and the offer they would get.
  3. Banner design. Pick bottom bar, top bar, corner card or modal, choose your colours and watch the live preview.
  4. API Config (optional). Add your API key so the AI can choose the incentive and write the copy. Without a key, offers are rules-based.
  5. Open your store in a private window, add a product to the cart, then move the pointer to the top edge of the window. Your offer appears.
An offer only appears when the cart's risk score reaches your threshold (default 55). If you test with a small cart and no exit intent, nothing may appear. That is the rules working. Use the simulator to see the score.

API key setup

The AI features are powered by the Google Gemini API using your own key.

  1. Open Google AI Studio → API keys and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it. It starts with AIza.
  3. In WordPress, go to DEV3 AI Smart Coupons → API Config, paste the key and click Validate & save. You should see "Connected — N models available".
The key is encrypted in your database (AES-256-GCM, bound to your site's salts) and is never sent to the browser. Agencies can put it in wp-config.php instead:
define( 'DEV3SC_GEMINI_API_KEY', 'AIza...' );

The recommended model is gemini-2.5-flash, which answers in about 2–4 seconds. Each offer uses roughly 1–2k tokens. The free tier is fine for testing. Busy stores should enable billing on the key's Google Cloud project.

How it works

1 · WatchA tiny script (about 4.5 KB gzipped, no jQuery) watches for exit intent, idle time and tab switches while the cart has items.
2 · ScoreWhen a trigger fires, the server computes a 0–100 abandonment risk score from the cart and the shopper's history.
3 · DecideAbove your threshold, the AI proposes an incentive and copy from anonymous cart context.
4 · ClampYour rules clamp every field: caps, margin floor, exclusions, budget and time window. If the AI fails, a rules-based offer is used.
5 · CouponA unique, single-use WooCommerce coupon is created. It expires with the countdown.
6 · ShowA banner shows the offer with a countdown and a one-click "Apply" button.

Each shopper gets at most one offer per day, checked by session, email and (optionally) IP. If they reload or move to checkout, they see the same offer again. A new one is never generated.

The AI never has the last word. Its answer is treated as untrusted input. Margin protection

Set Cost meta key to the product meta field that holds the unit cost:

Cost pluginMeta key
WooCommerce Cost of Goods (SkyVerge / Woo)_wc_cog_cost
Cost of Goods for WooCommerce (WPFactory)_alg_wc_cog_cost
ATUM Inventory_purchase_price
Anything elseYour own key, or the dev3sc_product_cost filter

For each cart line the plugin works out the room: line total − cost × quantity × (1 + minimum margin). The tightest line decides the largest discount ratio, because WooCommerce spreads percentage and fixed-cart discounts across lines in proportion to their price.

Worked example (minimum margin 15%)

LinePriceCostFloor (cost × 1.15)RoomMax discount
Hoodie60.0024.0027.6032.4054%
Sneakers120.0070.0080.5039.5032%
Backpack80.0072.0082.80−2.80excluded

The backpack has no room, so it is left out of the coupon: its ID goes into the coupon's excluded products. The coupon is limited to the hoodie and sneakers, and the offer can be at most 32% (then capped by your maximum percentage). The offer is also never more than your remaining daily budget. If the result is below your minimum, the next allowed type is tried, or no offer is made.

Products without a cost can be either discounted (your caps still apply) or never discounted.

Risk score

The score is a transparent, deterministic sum of points, clamped to 0–100. The same inputs always give the same score, and the simulator lists every factor.

The average order value is calculated from paid orders in the last 90 days and cached for 12 hours. Developers can change any weight with the dev3sc_risk_weights filter.

Triggers & consent

  • Exit intent (desktop): the pointer leaves through the top edge of the window, towards the tabs or the address bar.
  • Exit signals (mobile): a fast upward scroll (140 px within 350 ms, after scrolling at least 300 px down), or switching to another tab or app.
  • Idle time: no mouse, touch, scroll or key activity for the chosen number of seconds (default 30).
  • Minimum time on page: nothing fires during the first few seconds (default 4).
  • Run the tracker on: all pages, store pages only, or cart and checkout only.

The script reads WooCommerce's woocommerce_items_in_cart cookie and only reacts while the cart has items. It sends a request only when a trigger fires, and each trigger type fires at most once per page view.

Consent

  • Respect Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control (on by default): the tracker does not run for these visitors.
  • Require a consent cookie: enter the cookie name, or name=text that the value must contain. For example cookieyes-consent=analytics:yes or cmplz_marketing=allow.
  • Consent tools can also set window.dev3scConsent = true (allow) or false (block) before the script loads.

Banner design

  • Floating style: bottom bar, top bar, corner card or modal. Floating banners are position: fixed, so they never push the page around (no layout shift).
  • Inline on cart / checkout: the active offer is printed at the top of the cart and checkout in the page's HTML, so it adds no layout shift either. When the coupon is applied, the banner shows "Discount applied".
  • Countdown: counts to the coupon's real expiry time on the server, even if the shopper's clock is wrong.
  • Colours and radius with a contrast check (aim for at least 4.5:1).
  • Copy templates for rules-based offers, with the placeholders {discount} (for example "10% off", "$5 off", "free shipping") and {minutes}.
  • Brand voice is passed to the AI, for example "Warm, playful, never pushy".

Accessibility: banners are labelled regions; the modal is a proper dialog with focus moved to the button, a focus trap and Esc to close; new offers are announced to screen readers once; all buttons are at least 44 px high; reduced-motion preferences are respected. Shoppers can dismiss a floating banner, and it stays dismissed for the browsing session.

Flash sales

Flash sales are merchant-scheduled urgency bars shown to every visitor between a start and an end time, with a live countdown. Add one under DEV3 AI Smart Coupons → Flash sales:

  1. Set the start and end time (in your site's time zone).
  2. Describe the sale, for example "25% off all hoodies this weekend, code COZY25", pick a tone and click Generate copy with DEV3 AI. Edit the headline, message and button text as you like.
  3. Optionally show a coupon code you created in WooCommerce, and link the button to a page on your site.
  4. Choose top or bottom, and all pages, store pages or cart and checkout only.

The space for a top or bottom bar is reserved in the page's first paint, so the bar never covers your header. Flash sales do not track anyone and do not need consent. A flash sale does not create coupons; create the coupon itself under Marketing → Coupons.

Generated coupons

  • Normal WooCommerce coupons (Marketing → Coupons), marked as generated by the plugin.
  • Usage limit 1, limit per user 1, and an expiry date equal to the countdown.
  • Limited to the products whose margin was checked. The minimum spend is your rule; for fixed amounts it is the cart value at offer time.
  • Restricted to the customer's email when known (logged in, or entered at checkout).
  • Bound to the shopping session they were issued to. A shared or leaked code shows "This personal offer belongs to another shopping session".
  • Free-shipping offers need a Free shipping method that requires A valid free shipping coupon (or "either"/"both") in your shipping zones. Without one, free shipping is never offered.

Block & classic checkout

Both are supported. On the Cart and Checkout blocks, "Apply" uses the WooCommerce Store API, so the totals update instantly. On classic [woocommerce_cart] / [woocommerce_checkout] pages, and on any other page, "Apply" follows a secure one-click link (?dev3sc_apply=CODE&dev3sc_token=…). The token is bound to the shopper's session, applies the coupon and returns to the same page.

Caching & performance

  • Works with full-page caches (WP Rocket, LiteSpeed, Cloudflare APO and others). Cached pages contain only public settings and flash sales. A shopper's own offer comes from a REST request when a trigger fires, and is printed inline only on cart and checkout, which are never cached.
  • The tracker endpoint /wp-json/dev3sc/v1/track accepts same-origin requests only, is rate-limited per IP (12 per minute) and validates every field. No nonce is needed, so cached pages work.
  • At most one AI call per shopping session per 15 minutes, only when every rule already allows an offer. AI calls time out after 20 seconds and fall back to the rules.

Background jobs

The plugin uses Action Scheduler, which comes with WooCommerce (Tools → Scheduled Actions, group ai-smart-coupons):

  • dev3sc_sweep (hourly): marks expired offers and deletes generated coupons that expired more than N days ago and were never used. Used coupons are kept for your records.
  • dev3sc_budget_reset (daily at midnight, site time): resets the daily discount budget.

Updates

No licence key or activation is needed: every feature works as soon as the plugin is active. To update, download the latest version from your Envato Downloads page, then go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin and upload the zip. WordPress offers to replace the installed version and keeps all your settings and data.

FAQ

Will this train shoppers to wait for discounts?

Offers are rare by design: at most one per shopper per day, only above your risk threshold, and loyal recent customers score lower. Raise the threshold or lower the caps to be stricter, or allow only free shipping.

Does it slow my store down?

No. The storefront script is about 4.5 KB gzipped, loads in the footer, and makes no requests until a trigger fires.

Is it multisite / multi-currency compatible?

It works per site on multisite. Amounts are in your store's base currency.

Privacy

Sent to the Google Gemini API (only when a shopper qualifies for an offer, or when an admin uses an AI button): store currency, cart subtotal, item count, up to 8 product category names, your average order value, the risk score and its reasons, the trigger and page type, the customer segment (new or returning, number of previous orders), your limits and your brand-voice text. Never sent: names, emails, IP or street addresses, user IDs, product names or coupon codes.

Stored on your site: one row per offer in wp_dev3sc_offers (coupon, amounts, risk score, trigger, status, order). The session, email and IP are stored only as keyed one-way hashes, used to enforce one offer per day. In the shopper's browser, localStorage holds a first-visit timestamp, and sessionStorage holds a cart-view counter and the current offer. The plugin sets no cookies. Uninstalling with "Delete data on uninstall" removes the tables, settings and unused generated coupons.

Suggested privacy-policy text: "To offer timely discounts, our store analyses anonymous shopping-cart activity (cart value, time on page, whether you are leaving the page). When you qualify for an offer, anonymous cart information (no personal data) is processed by Google's Gemini API to choose the offer."
